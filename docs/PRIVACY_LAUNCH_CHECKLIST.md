# Classroom privacy and operations

Market Lab supports teacher-managed classroom use, including 12 assigned Fire tablets with intermittent internet. This document records operating practices; it is not an activation or approval gate. See [the classroom guide](CLASSROOM_OFFLINE.md) for setup.

- Use student aliases and unique PINs. Students need no email, legal name, phone number, location, or brokerage account.
- Keep teacher authentication, provider credentials, database credentials, and operator settings on the server.
- Keep each student assigned to one tablet/browser profile. The offline workspace stores that student's simulation portfolio, downloaded lessons, trade reflections, and a scoped device access key.
- Keep tablet backups private. Release a device only after its trades are synced before reassigning it.
- Do not clear Silk website storage or reset a tablet with unsynced work. Check all devices in after class before finalizing standings.
- A remote pause reaches disconnected tablets on their next rule download. Collect the physical devices when immediate classroom-wide control is needed.
- Use the teacher's roster, activity, exports, and device check-ins to manage the class. Preserve database recovery and an auditable accounting history.
- Keep operational contacts, retention practices, student-data export/deletion processes, and school communications current for the classroom's needs.
